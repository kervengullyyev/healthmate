# HealthMate — 90-second demo

For the minimalist interface, open `/` and click **Talk to Milo** once live voice is configured. For the full care journey below, open `/journey` and use **Try a demo**. No key or microphone is needed. All patient details, clinician identities and slots in this walkthrough are synthetic.

| Time | Action | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–15 s | Show home and Milo; choose Try a demo. | “HealthMate helps someone turn a health concern into a clear next step and prepare for a clinician conversation.” |
| 15–35 s | Use the five sample reply buttons to finish the interview. | “It asks about the pattern, severity, warning signs and relevant context. This is a fixed sample; unanswered details stay unknown.” |
| 35–50 s | Create the care plan. Read the suggested consultation and missing information. | “Recurring symptoms deserve professional assessment. The companion makes no diagnosis or safety guarantee.” |
| 50–65 s | Continue to appointments, select a slot and confirm the demo appointment. | “This demonstrates the handoff. These are sample clinicians and no real clinic is contacted.” |
| 65–85 s | Open Doctor brief, edit it, then show download or print. | “The patient can correct the summary and bring the reviewed brief to their own clinician.” |
| 85–90 s | Return home or reset. | “With a project key, the same journey starts with GPT-Live voice or text. The credential-free demo remains available for judging.” |

For a live demonstration, configure the private environment first, use a supported browser on localhost/HTTPS, grant microphone permission, and check audio playback. End the call before creating its plan. Do not present automated mocked checks as evidence of real account-backed audio working.
